# Brettgod1355

RuneLite plugins for Old School RuneScape. To install one, open RuneLite, click the Plugin Hub
(plug icon), search the name and click Install.

## [Advanced Map](https://github.com/Brettgod1355/Advanced-Map)

[![Advanced Map installs](https://img.shields.io/badge/dynamic/json?url=https%3A%2F%2Fbrettgod1355.github.io%2Fhub-installs%2Fdata%2Fbadges.json&query=%24.counts%5B%27advanced-map%27%5D&label=installs&color=dc8a00)](https://brettgod1355.github.io/hub-installs/)

A full-screen world map drawn from the game's own data, with smooth pan and zoom, a fuzzy search and icons you can
point at for their details.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Brettgod1355/Advanced-Map/main/docs/images/hero.webp" width="560" alt="The whole world on Advanced Map, maximized over the game">

- Always current: drawn from the map data your client already has, so a game update shows at once
- Over the game, filling the game view, or popped out into a window of its own for a second screen
- Find anything: places, shops, items, monsters, fishing spots and fairy rings, nearest to you first
- Point at a dungeon entrance for a small map of where you come out and every monster inside

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/advanced-map)

## [AFK Shipwrecks](https://github.com/Brettgod1355/AFK-Shipwrecks)

[![AFK Shipwrecks installs](https://img.shields.io/badge/dynamic/json?url=https%3A%2F%2Fbrettgod1355.github.io%2Fhub-installs%2Fdata%2Fbadges.json&query=%24.counts%5B%27cargo-hold-alert%27%5D&label=installs&color=dc8a00)](https://brettgod1355.github.io/hub-installs/)

An all-in-one salvaging plugin, whether you're fully AFK or working the hooks yourself.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/Brettgod1355/AFK-Shipwrecks/images/parking.webp" width="560" alt="Sailing into a double spot: the reach boxes round each wreck, one hook in, then both, and Parked">

- Timers for a full hold, each wreck sinking and the next one rising
- Alerts for a full hold, an empty hook or idle crew, with your own sound if you like
- Boxes on the water showing where to park in a double spot
- Inventory boxes for what to keep, deposit, alch or drop, and your next step on deck lit up

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/cargo-hold-alert) · [See it in action](https://github.com/Brettgod1355/AFK-Shipwrecks/wiki/Gallery)

## [Sidebar Favorites](https://github.com/Brettgod1355/Sidebar-Favorites)

[![Sidebar Favorites installs](https://img.shields.io/badge/dynamic/json?url=https%3A%2F%2Fbrettgod1355.github.io%2Fhub-installs%2Fdata%2Fbadges.json&query=%24.counts%5B%27sidebar-favorites%27%5D&label=installs&color=dc8a00)](https://brettgod1355.github.io/hub-installs/)

Your favorite panels. Your order. Your shortcuts.

- Keep the RuneLite panels you use most in one list, in your order
- Open one with a click or your own hotkey
- A gold star on the sidebar takes you back to your list

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/sidebar-favorites)
